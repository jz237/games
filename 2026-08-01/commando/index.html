COMMANDOHD

A TRIBUTE TO THE CLASSIC

MOVE WASD / arrows   FIRE Space / J / left mouse   GRENADE K / X / right mouse

Mouse aims when you move it. Hold fire with the keys to keep your facing and strafe.

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims & fires, A fires, B / RB throws a grenade.

Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb aims & fires, G throws a grenade.
