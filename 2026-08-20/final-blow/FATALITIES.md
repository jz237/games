# Final Blow 1.8 R-rated executions

Every Graphic Fatality follows the same focused cinematic grammar:

1. The victim is locked into a character-specific industrial restraint.
2. The winner activates their assigned signature special.
3. One arm or leg is explicitly severed. The complete limb is thrown into the
   scene, bounces once and remains beside the victim while the stump pumps blood.

The staging takes tonal influence from grim trap horror and 1990s arcade
Fatalities without reproducing a scene or device from another work.

The **Graphic Fatalities** option remains the master safety gate. Turning it off
keeps the cinematic camera and three-beat execution timing but removes severed
limbs, stump wounds, blood spray, gore fragments, pools and lens splatter.
